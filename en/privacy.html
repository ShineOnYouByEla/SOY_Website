Privacy in brief

This page sums up what happens to data when you use shineonyou.de. Only the full privacy policy is binding, and of that only the German original; where they differ, its text applies.

The essentials

  • No cookies. The site sets no cookies and does not measure usage – no web analytics, no advertising or tracking technology, no profiling.
  • A few local settings. The browser's localStorage only holds soy-theme (light or dark design), soy-lang (the language, if you switched it yourself), soy-channel-tip (the one-off notice about the WhatsApp channel) and soy-chat-teaser (the chat's one-off notice). It stays on your device and is not transmitted.
  • Local fonts. The fonts are served from this site's server; no third-party font library is called.
  • Server log files. The host records technical access data – this cannot be avoided when running a website.
  • Contact form. What you enter is processed to answer your enquiry. We need your consent before sending; an hCaptcha check protects against spam.
  • Appointment booking. Online booking runs through Cal.com. A connection is only made once you open it.
  • External links. The proWIN online shop, the WhatsApp channel and other third-party sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent – all in detail in the privacy policy. The controller is Manuela Zimmert, reachable at prowin.ela@web.de. You can also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.
